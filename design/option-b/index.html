01 / Timebase

X 0 → 12 · STEP 1 WK

The semester runs on a fixed timebase. Each tick is one week, and each week carries the same shape: a shared lecture that installs a method, a stream session that stress-tests it, and a short return that shows what moved. Weeks 04, 08 and 12 are calibration points — no new material, only evidence that the previous three weeks landed.

02 / Channels

3 CH · COMMON BUS · INDEPENDENT GAIN

Every student sits on the common bus — the shared lecture — and then picks one channel for the semester. Channels are not difficulty tiers. They differ in what counts as evidence, and that difference is the curriculum. Colour coding here is a convenience; the channel letter is the authority.

Channel A · Stream A

Field and interview

You go and ask people. Stream A treats the transcript as the primary artefact and the summary as a derivative you must be able to defend line by line. Expect to discard your first framing by week five.

Weekly output is a coded excerpt plus a note on what you got wrong in the asking.

04 / Operating notes

POLICY · PLAIN LANGUAGE

How to fail this unit

The reliable way to fail is to hide the middle of your work. A folio that contains only the finished artefact reads as a folio with no process in it, and process is what the rubric measures. Show the version you abandoned and say why you abandoned it.

The second reliable way is to treat the shared lecture as optional. Channels diverge fast, and the lecture is the only place the three of them stay commensurable. Miss three and you will be arguing from a different set of terms than the people marking you.

Extensions are granted, not negotiated. Ask before the due date, name a new date, and the new date becomes the due date. Asking afterwards is a different conversation and a worse one.

Standing tolerances
  • LateSeventy-two hours, uncapped and unpenalised. After that, ask.
  • AI usePermitted and logged. An unlogged tool is the misconduct, not the tool.
  • ReworkAny weekly return may be resubmitted once, up to week eleven.
  • AccessEvery session has a transcript. Every deck has a text version.
  • Channel swapOnce, before the end of week three, no reason required.
