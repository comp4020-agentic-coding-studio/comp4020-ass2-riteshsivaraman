A twelve-week course in making your working legible.

Most of what you learn in a studio or a lab never leaves the room. It stays in drafts, in half-finished objects, in the version you did not show anyone. This unit treats that material as the actual subject.

You will keep a record of your own working for twelve weeks and learn to read it the way someone else would. Nothing here is graded on how the work turned out. Everything is graded on whether the process can be followed.

Part three of fourThree streams

One shared lecture, three parallel sessions. You choose in week one and stay. They cover the same ground through different material and are assessed identically.

A

The written record

Tue 14:00 · Room 2.14 · 22 places

Everything here ends as text you could hand to someone who was not in the room. You will write more than you expect and keep less than you would like.

The recurring exercise is the stranger test: give a draft to someone with no context, say nothing, and write down their first question word for word.

B

The spoken record

Tue 14:00 · Studio 1 · 18 places

Two takes of ninety seconds every week, recorded. You keep both, annotate the transcript afterwards, and bring the annotation rather than the audio.

Nobody is marked on delivery. The evidence is the distance between the first take and the second, and what you did in the gap.

C

The made record

Tue 14:00 · Workshop B · 16 places

You bring one finished object and the group works out what it actually demonstrates, which is rarely what it was assigned to demonstrate.

The gap between those two readings holds most of the unclaimed evidence in this stream. Keep the failures; they carry more of it than the good version.

Part four of fourPracticalities

The policies you will actually need, written out rather than linked to.

Extensions

Ask before the due date and you get five days with no documentation. Ask after it and it becomes a formal application. The difference is administrative, not moral.

Attendance

The lecture is recorded; the stream session is not, because it depends on the people in the room. Stream attendance is counted and you may miss two.

Switching streams

Possible until the end of week three, subject to places. After that the portfolio assumes continuity and switching costs you more than it saves.

Using outside work

Encouraged. If you are already making the thing, bring it. Declare where it came from and be specific about which part of it is new for this unit.
