The mechanical problem02

Two passes,
never the same
twice

The sheet goes through the press once for the first ink and again for the second. Between those two passes the paper has been gripped, bent, warmed and released.

  • Grain direction decides which way it stretches
  • Humidity changes the dimension between passes
  • The gripper edge is the only fixed reference, and it is one edge

Perfect registration is not a default that sometimes fails. It is an achievement that usually fails a little.

The turn04

Three readings,
and they conflict

The same off-register sheet supports three incompatible interpretations. All three are defensible. Using one without noticing you chose is not.

One
Noise — characterise it, subtract it, move on.
Two
Signature — it identifies the press, the hand, the session.
Three
Resource — induce it deliberately, at a magnitude you pick.

Pick one aloud today

Reading one05

01

Noise

Treat the offset as error: measure its distribution across many sheets, report a mean and a spread, then design the process so the spread stops mattering.

Strong when you need the thing to work a thousand times and nobody will look at any individual sheet.

Costly when the variation was the only record you had of how the process actually behaves — subtract it and you have thrown away your instrument.

The engineer’s readingFold 05

Reading two06

02

Signature

Treat the offset as an identifier. A press drifts in a characteristic direction; so does a hand, a jig, a Tuesday afternoon. The imperfection is how you attribute the artefact.

Strong when you need to know who or what made something, and the maker had no interest in telling you.

Costly when you start reading intention into drift. A signature identifies a source. It does not explain a choice.

Reading three07

03

Resource

Treat the offset as something to induce. Choose a direction and a magnitude, print it on purpose, and own the result as a decision rather than an accident.

Strong when the misalignment does work the aligned version cannot — depth, vibration, the sense of two things nearly agreeing.

Costly when it becomes a way of not having to register anything. An induced offset you cannot reproduce on demand is still an accident.
