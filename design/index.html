Three design directions

Standalone demos, abstract placeholder content, deliberately blind to the existing front end. Open each at 1920×1080 and then at 390×844 — the phone view is where they separate. Each option carries notes on its palette, motion inventory and port risks.

Option A

Kinetic Manifesto

Typography is the layout. Variable-font mass reacting to scroll, horizontal movement, one high-voltage accent on paper white. Loud, editorial, no cards.
