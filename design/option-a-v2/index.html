§03

Three streams, one lecture

You pick a stream in week one and stay in it. They cover the same ground through different material, and they are assessed identically.

A

The written record

Tuesdays 14:00 · Room 2.14 · 22 places

Everything in this stream ends up as text you could hand to someone who was not in the room. You will write more than you expect and keep less of it than you would like.

The recurring exercise is the stranger test: give a draft to someone with no context and record, verbatim, the first question they ask.

B

The spoken record

Tuesdays 14:00 · Studio 1 · 18 places

Two takes of ninety seconds, every week, recorded. You keep both, annotate the transcript, and bring the annotations rather than the audio.

Nobody is marked on delivery. The evidence is the gap between the first take and the second, and what you did in between.

C

The made record

Tuesdays 14:00 · Workshop B · 16 places

You bring one finished object and the group works out what it actually demonstrates, which is rarely what it was assigned to demonstrate.

The gap between those two readings is where most of the unclaimed evidence in this stream lives. Keep the failures; they carry more of it.

§04

Running notes

The policies you will actually need, in the order you will need them.

Extensions

Ask before the due date and you get five days, no documentation. Ask after and it becomes a formal application. The difference is entirely administrative.

Attendance

The lecture is recorded; the stream session is not. Stream attendance is the only place attendance is counted, and you may miss two.

Switching streams

Possible until the end of week three, subject to places. After that the portfolio assumes continuity, and switching costs you more than it saves.

Using outside work

Encouraged. If you are already making the thing, bring it. Declare where it came from and what part of it is new for this unit.
