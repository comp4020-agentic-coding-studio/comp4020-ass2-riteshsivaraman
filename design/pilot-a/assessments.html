Assessments

Each assessment’s brief and rubric sit on this one page — A1 through A4 — rather than split out separately, so following one link gets you everything you need for a given piece: what it submits, when it’s due, and exactly how it’s weighted.

Four assessments, not three. Keeping the capstone under a 50% ceiling while leaving the earlier pieces honestly weighted needed a fourth, smaller component — that component is peer critique, graded on submitted written critiques, never on attendance or airtime.

20 A1 · Role Teardown and Gap Map
25 A2 · Performance Materials
10 A3 · Peer Critique Contribution
45 A4 · Capstone: Applications in the Wild
20 + 25 + 10 + 45 = 100. Largest is 45, under the 50% ceiling.

A3 — Peer Critique Contribution

Week 11 Weight 10% Due Fri 14 May 2027, 5:00pm
Submits
The three most substantial written critiques the student gave across the semester — from any of weeks 3, 6, 8 or 11 — each with a short note on what it changed in the recipient’s work, countersigned by the recipient.

Graded on submitted written critiques only, never on attendance or on talking in a tutorial.

A4 — Capstone: Applications in the Wild

assessments/04-capstone-applications
Week 12 Weight 45% Due Wed 26 May 2027, 5:00pm
Submits
The application materials as actually sent, the consolidated twelve-entry reflection log, one interview debrief, the ninety-day ramp plan, and the performance-versus-genuine audit.

Graded on process artefacts only. Never on outcome, never on offer, never on the prestige of the organisation.

Students apply to roles they are actually pursuing anyway — applying to a role with no intention of taking it is not permitted. By the end of the week 9 tutorial, every student declares an interview route: a real one, or the Practice Interview Partner Pool, coordinated by Margot Tse, of recruiters and practitioners who have given signed written consent to conduct a practice interview knowing it is a teaching exercise. The debrief below is graded identically either way — there is no mark difference between routes. Full framing of the pool and the consent it runs on is on Policies.

Marking — weighted · graded on process, never on outcome
CriterionWeight
Gap articulation under real conditions25
Performance awareness — what was performed, what was genuine, and why25
Adaptation under real friction25
Completeness and honesty of the process record15
Interview debrief quality10
Total100
