Three field streams · one tutorial each, every week

The streams

Lectures are shared by everyone. Tutorials split into three streams, and the split runs on how a field actually screens candidates — not on faculty or discipline. That is why a health-informatics student and a civil engineer end up in the same room: both get screened with a technical exercise, whatever their degree is called.

Pick by how your target roles screen you, not by what your degree is called.

§01

Technical Practice

Convenor

Dr Anselm Pike

For the fields where the performance is competence, and someone will eventually ask you to open a terminal in front of them.

Fields
Software, engineering, data and analytics, applied and environmental science, health and biomedical technology, IT operations.
Pick it if
Your target roles screen with a technical exercise, a take-home task, or a portfolio of things that must actually run.
§02

Creative and Communications Practice

Convenor

Nadia Ferrante

For the fields where the performance is taste, and the portfolio is both the evidence and the alibi.

Fields
Design, media production, writing and editing, marketing and communications, architecture, arts and cultural administration.
Pick it if
Your target roles ask to see a body of work and judge it on judgement rather than on correctness.
§03

Commercial and Public Practice

Convenor

Dr Sunil Raman

For the fields where the performance is judgement, and nobody will ever ask to see the spreadsheet.

Fields
Consulting, finance, accounting, law, policy and public service, management, NGO and program delivery.
Pick it if
Your target roles screen with written selection criteria, competency panels, or assessment centres.

Cross-stream tutorial attendance is encouraged, never assessed. Two weeks deliberately break the stream walls: week 6 ends with a cross-stream lap of the gallery walk, and week 12’s viva admits cross-stream observers.
