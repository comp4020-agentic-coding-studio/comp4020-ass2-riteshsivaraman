Margot came out of recruitment into the university deliberately, having spent
long enough inside the process to want to explain it to the people it happens
to. She coordinates the **Practice Interview Partner Pool** — recruiters and
practitioners who have signed written consent to conduct a practice interview
for a course in which the interview is a teaching exercise. She keeps that
paperwork, and she is the reason nobody unconsenting is approached.

See Margot about three things: the **week-9 interview route declaration** and
joining the pool, **extensions and special consideration**, and **any
application process that is making you feel unwell** — which is common, not a
character flaw, and an ordinary conversation rather than a disclosure.

She does not mark capstone debriefs for interviews she facilitated; that
separation is structural, not a courtesy. She delivers the week-7 and week-9
lectures, and the pool's consent rules are set out on the
[policies page](/policies/).
