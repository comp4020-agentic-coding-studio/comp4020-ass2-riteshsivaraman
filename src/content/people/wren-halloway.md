Wren spent ten years on the other side of the table — graduate panels, mid-
career shortlists, and the part of the process where three hundred documents
are reduced to twelve in an afternoon. She built SLOP3841 out of the pattern
she could not stop seeing there: capable people losing rooms they could have
held, because nobody had told them the room was a performance and that
performing well is a learnable, legitimate skill.

She opens every semester by naming which parts of her own job she is still
performing, with examples. It is the only demonstration of the method that
cannot be faked.

Wren convenes the course and teaches weeks 1, 5, 11 and 12, plus the week-8
red team with Anselm Pike. Bring her course-level questions, anything a stream
convenor has escalated, and any suspicion that a session is teaching the wrong
side of the line between framing and fabrication.
