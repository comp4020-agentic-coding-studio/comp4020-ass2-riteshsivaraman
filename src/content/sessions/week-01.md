You will spend ninety minutes this week arguing with your own CV, and you will
lose. That is the intended outcome.

## Bring

Three real job advertisements for roles you would actually take, and whatever
materials you currently have — however embarrassing, and embarrassing is the
median. A half-finished portfolio is a better starting position than a polished
one, because there is less to unlearn.

Do not tidy anything before you arrive. Tidying before an audit is the exact
habit this course exists to interrupt.

## In the room

Your convenor puts one column on the board labelled **claim** and one labelled
**what I could show, today, in ninety seconds**. You fill both. The gap between
them is your **receipt debt**, and week 4 is about paying it down; this week you
only have to look at it.

Then, in pairs, you read your claims aloud to someone who does not know you.
They ask "show me" exactly once per claim. Anything you cannot answer gets a
mark beside it. Nobody is being caught out — the marks are the deliverable.

Your stream runs its own version of the second half:

- **Technical Practice** audits a CV against one repository. A claim survives
  if it points at something that runs.
- **Creative and Communications Practice** audits a portfolio piece by piece,
  separating *finished* from *in progress* honestly, including the pieces that
  have been in progress for two years.
- **Commercial and Public Practice** audits a LinkedIn profile and one cover
  letter, on the single test of whether a claim survives one "show me".

## Leaving with

Dossier §1, your Baseline Inventory: every claim, its evidence status, and
nothing aspirational. Dossier §2, three target advertisements, chosen and not
yet analysed — week 2 takes them apart.

Nothing this week is graded. The point of an ungraded first week is that you
have no incentive to perform for it, which is the only week of the semester
where that is true, and it is why the inventory you write now is the one you
will trust in week 12.

## A note on what this course is asking of you

The course is built on a distinction it will hold to for twelve weeks:
**framing what is true is professional practice; claiming what is not is
fraud.** Every technique taught here lives on one side of that line. If a
session ever seems to be teaching you the other thing, you have misread it, or
we have taught it badly — say so, and we will fix the session.
