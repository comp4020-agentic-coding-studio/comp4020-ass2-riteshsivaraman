Hand your Dossier to the person on your left, take the one arriving from your
right, and spend fifteen minutes reading a stranger's semester. Then present it.
Not your own — theirs — to a practitioner from your field who is in the tutorial
room for the whole ninety minutes.

## Why you present somebody else's

Two reasons, and both are load-bearing.

The first is that the critique becomes usable. A practitioner responding to *the
case* says what they think, in the register they use at work. A practitioner
responding to *you, visibly hoping* says something kinder and less true. The
swap puts a metre of distance in the room and the honesty arrives through the
gap.

The second is that it survives. You will hear your own materials criticised
while not holding them, which is the only comfortable way anyone has found to
hear it. You are also the one advocating for the work, so you arrive having read
it properly — which is more attention than most of your materials have had from
anyone other than their author.

## The guests

One practitioner per stream, hosted by that stream's convenor, from that
stream's field. They are here as practitioners rather than as recruiters: the
brief we give them is to say what the first ninety days in their team actually
looks like for the person described in this Dossier, and where the competence
lag would land.

They are not assessing anyone and they are not hiring anyone. If you want a
question answered that is embarrassing to ask a person who might employ you,
this is the room and this is the hour.

## Building the ramp

The second half is written, not spoken. You take your own Dossier back with the
practitioner's response attached and build a ninety-day ramp out of your
[week 3](/sessions/week-03/) gap map: what you close before you start, what you
close in the first month by asking, what you close by month three by doing, and
what you tell your manager in week one that you cannot yet do.

The last item is the one people delete. Leave it in. A ramp with no declared
weakness is a document that will be quietly wrong by day ten.
