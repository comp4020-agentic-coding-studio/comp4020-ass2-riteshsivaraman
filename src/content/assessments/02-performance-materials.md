This is the pack you would actually send. Not a generic pack with the company
name swapped in — a pack fitted to one named target, with a written record of
every decision you made about how it looks.

## The facts

- **Submit** the fitted materials plus the Varnish Note, as one bundle, through
  the course submission point.
- **Due** Friday 23 April 2027, 5:00 pm (end of week 8).
- **Worth** 25% of the course.
- **Marked** against the four weighted criteria at the foot of this page.

## What goes in

Name the target first — one role, one organisation, from your
[A1](/assessments/01-role-teardown/) shortlist. Then four things fitted to it.

1. **A CV or résumé**, rewritten for this target rather than for everyone.
2. **A cover letter, pitch or criteria response**, in whatever form that target
   actually reads.
3. **One field-appropriate artefact.** Technical Practice: a repository front
   page and README. Creative and Communications: a portfolio case study in
   sequence. Commercial and Public: a one-page capability brief.
4. **The Varnish Note.** Every presentational decision you made, what it
   reveals, and what it conceals. Both halves. "I moved the group project to
   the top because it is the only thing with a user count, which also moves my
   solo work down the page" is an entry. "Improved layout" is not.

## Where it comes from

[Week 5](/sessions/week-05/) gives you the three anecdotes the letter is built
from. [Week 6](/sessions/week-06/) is the varnish week — the
[lecture](/lectures/week-06/) and its [deck](/decks/week-06-varnish/) set out
the four thin-varnish moves and the four thick-varnish tells, and the gallery
walk puts your draft on a wall with written critique beside it. Use the
teaching break to build, not to polish. [Week 8](/sessions/week-08/) red-teams
the pack inside your stream and times how long it takes a peer to find your
weakest claim; the diff note you leave with is what you revise against.

## What separates a strong submission

Fitness is 30 marks and it is decided in the first fifteen seconds of reading.
A marker who cannot tell from the top third which role this is for has already
priced that in.

Evidence is 25, and it is the same test as A1: can a stranger verify it without
you in the room. Receipt debt you carried out of week 4 is expected to be paid
down by now, not restated.

Performance awareness is another 25, and it is the Varnish Note doing the work.
The note is graded on honesty and specificity, not on the materials being
unvarnished. Varnish is not a lie; it is a claim about where to look. An
unflattering true entry scores; a flattering vague one does not.

Craft is 20. Typos, broken links, a README that does not say what the thing
does, a case study with no problem statement — these are cheap marks to lose.

## The line this sits on

Fitting materials to a target means selecting and framing what is true. It
never means inventing, inflating a role you did not hold, or claiming a team's
output as yours. Attribution is checked here and again at the capstone, and
overclaiming is an academic integrity matter under the
[policies page](/policies/).
