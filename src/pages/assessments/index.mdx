Four pieces, weighted 20 + 25 + 10 + 45, summing to 100. They are one artefact
submitted four times: the Dossier gains a numbered section every week, goes in
at weeks 4 and 8, is red-teamed, and closes as the capstone.

The capstone is the applications you were going to send anyway. It is graded on
the process record — never on the outcome, never on whether anyone said yes, and
never on the name of the organisation.
