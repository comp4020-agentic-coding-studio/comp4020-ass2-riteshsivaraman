A convenor, three stream convenors, and a practice fellow. Between them they
have spent enough time on hiring panels to have opinions about your CV, which is
the entire staffing rationale.

Course-level questions go to the convenor; anything about your own tutorial goes
to the convenor of your stream; the week-9 interview route, extensions, and any
application process that is making you feel unwell go to the practice fellow.
