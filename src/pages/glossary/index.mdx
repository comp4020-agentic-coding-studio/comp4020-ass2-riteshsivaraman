Twelve terms, one per teaching week. A course that has invented its own jargon
for career advice is, admittedly, part of the joke. The glosses are not: each
one attaches to something you have to be able to do, and each links back to the
week that teaches it.

They also refer to each other. Receipt debt is what the plausibility budget is
spent on; seamwork is the audit of the truthfloor you set in week 1.
